Why they are all so small

Each of these started as something I wanted for myself and could not find without signing up for an account, handing over my data, or being sold a subscription.

So they are deliberately narrow. They do not sync, they do not notify you more than they need to, and they do not collect anything. Whatever you type stays on your phone.
