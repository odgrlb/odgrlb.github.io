Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy covers every iPhone app published by Apps by Ogi, including Yearly Highlights, Leksi, Know Thy Day, and Grounded.

What we collect

Nothing.

These apps do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's own private storage. It is never sent anywhere. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

Because the data is stored only on your device, deleting an app deletes its data permanently. Your data is included in an iCloud or iTunes device backup if you have one enabled, but that backup is between you and Apple — it does not pass through us.

Analytics and tracking

None. These apps contain no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party components that collect data.

Children

These apps do not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
